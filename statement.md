# Project Statement: Smart Laundry Tracker & Amount Calculator

## Problem Statement
Managing personal laundry expenses manually often leads to inaccurate calculations, misplaced receipts, and difficulty tracking expenses over time. Existing solutions are either too complex or embedded within paid enterprise systems. There is a need for a modular, efficient, and user-friendly system to manage daily laundry logs, maintain adaptable rate structures, and auto-generate receipts.

## Scope of Project
The **Smart Laundry Tracker & Amount Calculator** is a lightweight, terminal-based CLI application built in Python. It handles rate card updates, item logging, cost calculations, dynamic receipts, and local JSON-based data persistence.

## Target Users
* College students and hostel residents managing personal expenses.
* Small-scale laundry vendors needing quick receipts and rate administration.
* Households tracking recurring domestic expenses.

## High-Level Features
* **Rate Management:** Add, edit, and dynamic display of item unit prices.
* **Order Logging:** Add laundry items with real-time price lookup and input validation.
* **Receipt & Summary Generation:** Calculate sub-totals, grand totals, and total piece counts in a formatted tabular view.
* **Data Persistence:** Store item rates and persistent logs across CLI execution sessions.
